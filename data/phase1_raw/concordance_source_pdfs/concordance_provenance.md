# Concordance Source PDFs & Gazette Provenance

This directory stores references and extracted gazette data from official Government of India sources:
1. **Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA) Comparative Concordance Tables** (IPC 1860 vs. BNS 2023).
2. **The Gazette of India: Extraordinary (Part II — Section 1)**, No. 45, New Delhi, December 25, 2023 (Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023, Act No. 45 of 2023).
3. **The Gazette of India: Extraordinary (Part II — Section 1)**, No. 46, New Delhi, December 25, 2023 (Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023, Act No. 46 of 2023).
4. **The Gazette of India: Extraordinary (Part II — Section 1)**, No. 47, New Delhi, December 25, 2023 (Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023, Act No. 47 of 2023).

Processed machine-readable concordance mappings are stored in `data/02_ground_truth/concordance_v1.csv`.
